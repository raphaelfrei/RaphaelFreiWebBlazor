Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

1. Personal Data & Registration

Chromaku does not require any account creation, login, or registration. We do not directly collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.

2. Advertisements

Chromaku uses third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) to support the free development and availability of the game, including optional rewarded ads (to obtain hints or extra lives).

These advertising partners may collect and process standard anonymous device identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS or GAID on Android), coarse IP location, and technical device data to serve and frequency-cap advertisements, in full compliance with Apple App Store and Google Play Store policies.

3. No In-App Purchases

The game does not process in-app purchases. All color palettes, game modes, and daily puzzles are unlocked through natural gameplay progression.

4. Local Storage & Game Progress

Your game board progress, scores, daily challenge streaks, and unlocked color palettes are saved exclusively to your device's local storage. This data is never sent to external developer servers.

5. Supported Platforms

Chromaku is officially distributed through the Apple App Store and the Google Play Store, adhering strictly to the privacy and security guidelines of each platform.

6. Contact & Support

If you have questions, feedback, or inquiries regarding privacy, please contact:

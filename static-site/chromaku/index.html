Game Mechanics

How It Works

Classic mathematical Sudoku rules adapted for color perception and spatial reasoning.

01

No Repeated Colors

Each horizontal row, vertical column, and 3x3 block must contain all 9 unique colors without repetition in any direction.

02

Draft Notes & Hints

Use the pencil draft tool to mark candidate colors in undecided cells, and request hints whenever you reach an impasse.

03

Daily Challenge & Lives

Play a brand-new exclusive puzzle every 24 hours. Stay focused: every mistake costs one of your 3 lives per game.

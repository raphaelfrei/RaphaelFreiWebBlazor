Pastory

Your clipboard, upgraded.

Have you ever copied something important, only to accidentally overwrite it a second later? Pastory is the lightweight, native clipboard manager for macOS that remembers everything you copy, so you don't have to.

Sitting quietly in your menu bar, Pastory keeps a secure, easily accessible history of your copied texts, links, and snippets. It stays completely out of your way until the exact moment you need it.

Key Features

Always at Hand

Lives exclusively in your macOS menu bar. No messy dock icons or heavy windows interrupting your workflow.

Native

Built natively with Swift and SwiftUI to ensure fluid performance and minimal battery consumption.

Privacy First

Everything you copy is stored locally on your Mac. No external servers, no cloud tracking, and no accounts required.

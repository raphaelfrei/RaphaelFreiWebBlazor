Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

At Pastory, your privacy is our top priority. We believe that what you copy should stay exactly where it belongs: on your own device.

1. Local Storage Only

Pastory operates entirely locally on your macOS device. Everything you copy—texts, links, snippets, and images—is stored directly on your computer's local storage.

2. No Cloud Tracking or Analytics

We do not track, collect, or analyze the contents of your clipboard.

3. No Accounts Required

Pastory does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information.

4. macOS Permissions

To function correctly, Pastory requires specific macOS permissions to read your system clipboard.

5. Your Data, Your Control

You have full control over your data. You can clear your entire clipboard history at any time.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we decide to change our privacy policy, we will post those changes on this page.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
